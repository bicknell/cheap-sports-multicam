# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in cheap-sports-multicam!

## Reporting problems

If something doesn't work, please open an issue on
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/bicknell/cheap-sports-multicam/issues).
Include what you ran, what you expected, and what happened instead.

## Submitting fixes

Pull requests with fixes are welcome. Please open them against `main`.

By submitting a pull request you agree that your contribution is licensed
under the project's [BSD 3-Clause License](LICENSE).
